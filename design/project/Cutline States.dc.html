OurCut · storyline timeline
Renamed to OurCut and built on the Windows (Fluent) version from turn 2. The timeline is now an assembled storyline: clips sit back to back in output order, and each one is a single rounded block with its name and duration, a film strip, and its own waveform directly underneath. Drag a block to reorder it. When a clip is selected, trim handles appear on its edges and the clips after it shift to close the gap. A thin source bar above shows where each clip comes from in the original file, along with silences and scene cuts. Click it to scrub the source.
